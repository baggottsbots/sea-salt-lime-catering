Our Story

Three concepts, one coastal table

Sea Salt & Lime Catering Co. was created by experienced Lowcountry restaurant teams who know that the best gatherings happen around great food.

We bring together three culinary styles under one roof: fresh coastal seafood, time-honored Lowcountry tradition and bold Latin flavor. Choose one, or mix all three into a menu that is entirely your own.

From seafood boils and Southern-inspired spreads to Latin favorites and custom menus, we create celebrations that feel generous, polished and effortless.
